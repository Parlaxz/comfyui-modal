Making and Connecting Workspaces in Modal.

Step 1

1. Open modal and press the list of workspaces in the top left. press "Manage Workspaces"

Step 2

2. Press "Docs".

Step 3

3. Go to the new workspace you just made.

Step 4

4. Go to the "API tokens & service users" section.

Step 5

5. Press "New Token", name it, and create it.

Step 6

6. Copy the full key text, then extract the 2 parts you need. You need the ID, which starts with "ak-", and the secret, which starts with "as-".

Step 6b

Add card to modal

Step 6c

set spend limit to $29.00 or $29.50

Step 7

7. Go back to the Modal ComfyUI app and open the Studio.

Step 8

8. Add a new workspace.

Step 9

9. Enter a clear label, such as "ComfyUI Production", then press Save.

Step 10

10. Select the workspace you want to use, then press "Set Active".

Step 11

11. Press "Install from Manifest", then wait for the workspace deployment and model installation to finish. It might take 10-20 minutes on the first install because it has to deploy and build the app. That's expected.

Step 12

12. Watch the deployment status in the Deployment tab. The first deploy may error when many custom nodes are installed. Press "Redeploy and Restart" and repeat until the deployment completes successfully. It is common for this to take a second or third deploy.

Step 13

13. Once deployment is done, refresh the Chrome page, go back to "Install from Manifest", and install the required stuff.
