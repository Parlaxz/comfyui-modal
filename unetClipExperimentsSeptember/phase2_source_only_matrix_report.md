## Findings

- QD1 is consistently inferior for both models.
- QD2 is the strongest stable region for UNET across block sizes.
- CLIP has a shallow QD2/QD4 region, with the best median at 256 MiB/QD2.
- QD8 does not restore the expected scaling in this corrected source-only
  matrix; it is slower than QD2 in every displayed model/block family.
- Large isolated samples remain visible in raw artifacts and were not removed:
  UNET 32 MiB/QD1, 32 MiB/QD4, 32 MiB/QD8, and 256 MiB/QD8.
